Search or filter, then open a signal to see how we qualify it, what we saw in the log, and the change to make in the warehouse and in Lattice.

How we read the log
Question
One query that Lattice ran and that finished. Cache hits, dry runs and EXPLAIN calls are left out, because they put no load on the tables.
Question shape
A question with its values taken out. "Revenue by month for EMEA" and "for APAC" share one shape.
Cost
The database time of a question. With warehouse history joined in through the query tag, cost can also be data scanned or credits billed.
Window
The period a signal looks at. A signal that holds in three windows in a row is stronger than one that holds once.
Thresholds
Every number in a qualifying rule is a default that the maintainer can tune.
Data needed
Query log what Lattice asked: questions, joins, filters, columns and timings.
Warehouse metadata what the tables already are: sizes, indexes, partition keys, column types, unique keys and distinct counts.
Warehouse history who else reads the tables and what it really costs: every tool's queries, loads and billing, joined to the log through the query tag.
